**PRIVACY NOTICE**

**Last updated October 29, 2026**

This notice covers the NeewerTV container image and the code in this repository.
It is short, because there is almost nothing to say.

**1. WHAT INFORMATION DO WE COLLECT?**

**In Short:** None. NeewerTV does not collect, transmit, or store any personal
information, and it has no way to do so.

The container:

* makes **no outbound connection except to the MQTT broker you configure.** There
  is no telemetry endpoint, no crash reporting, no update check, and no
  phone-home of any kind in the code. You can confirm this yourself: the only
  network client in the dependency tree is the MQTT library, and
  `docker network disconnect` on the bridge breaks nothing except MQTT.
* talks to **one Bluetooth light strip** through the D-Bus socket you mount into
  it. That is a local radio link, not an internet connection — the device has no
  network interface of its own.
* keeps **no database and no persistent volume.** State is held in memory and
  lost on restart. There is no analytics, no session tracking, no user accounts,
  and no identifiers of any kind.
* writes **logs to stdout** and nothing else. They are yours: they stay on your
  host, and they are as private as your `docker logs` are.

The optional web interface serves static pages generated by the daemon itself. It
sets no cookies, loads no external fonts, scripts, or images, and talks to nothing
outside your network.

**2. WILL YOUR INFORMATION BE SHARED WITH ANYONE?**

**In Short:** We have nothing to share, and no mechanism to share it.

There is no backend. The maintainers never see your MQTT topics, your device, or
your logs, and could not access them if asked. There is no company, no server, and
no account system.

Two things remain your responsibility:

* **Your MQTT broker.** The daemon publishes and subscribes to topics on a broker
  you run. Whatever that broker records is governed by your setup, not by us.
* **Your Home Assistant instance.** Discovery payloads and state are ordinary MQTT
  traffic, and are retained or logged according to how you configured that broker.

**3. DO WE USE COOKIES AND OTHER TRACKING TECHNOLOGIES?**

**In Short:** No. The web interface sets no cookies and embeds no third-party
content. There is no advertising, no analytics, and no fingerprinting, so there is
nothing to opt out of.

**4. HOW LONG DO WE KEEP YOUR INFORMATION?**

**In Short:** We hold nothing. The daemon keeps its last commands in memory only,
and only so an alert can put the strip back the way it found it. Restart the
container and that is gone.

**5. HOW DO WE KEEP YOUR INFORMATION SAFE?**

**In Short:** By not having any.

The container does hold a **Bluetooth link** and, depending on your configuration,
an **MQTT credential**. Both come from your environment or your compose file and
live only in the process. Neither is written to disk by the daemon.

Be aware of the threat model that does exist. The BLE link to the strip is
unauthenticated and unencrypted, as it is with the vendor app: any device within
radio range can control the strip. That is a property of the hardware, not
something this software can fix, and it does not involve your personal data. See
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for the deployment expectations that follow from it.

**6. WHAT ARE YOUR PRIVACY RIGHTS?**

**In Short:** There is nothing for us to disclose, correct, or delete, because we
never received anything. Rights over data you hold locally are exercised by you,
on your own machine.

If you believe this notice is inaccurate — for example you have found a network
call we said does not exist — that is a bug, and we would like to hear about it.
Please open an issue.

**7. CONTROLS FOR DO-NOT-TRACK FEATURES**

No tracking exists, so no signal is needed. `DNT` and Global Privacy Control are
irrelevant to a program with no analytics.

**8. DO CALIFORNIA RESIDENTS HAVE SPECIFIC PRIVACY RIGHTS?**

**In Short:** The CCPA/CPRA obligations attach to a business that collects
personal information. We do not collect personal information, sell it, share it,
or retain it, so there is nothing to disclose, opt out of, or delete. No category
of personal information has been collected in the preceding twelve months.

**9. DO WE MAKE UPDATES TO THIS NOTICE?**

Yes, if the software ever changes in a way that matters here — for example if
someone contributes an optional feature that talks to a third-party service. Any
such feature would be **opt-in, off by default, and documented in this file
before it ships**. The date at the top changes when this notice does.

**10. HOW CAN YOU CONTACT US ABOUT THIS NOTICE?**

Open an issue at
<https://github.com/Hyper-Dragon/NeewerTV/issues/new/choose>.

**11. HOW CAN YOU REVIEW, UPDATE OR DELETE THE DATA WE COLLECT FROM YOU?**

There is no data to review, update or delete. To remove everything the daemon
has ever held: `docker rm -f neewertv`. That is the whole procedure.
